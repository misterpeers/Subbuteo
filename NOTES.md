## Suggested first drill: shot blocking

- Set up the ball, attacking piece, goal, and one defending piece.
- Let an attacking flick settle, then allow one defensive block-flick before
  the next attack. The defender positions to cover the next shot lane; it does
  not try to contact the moving ball.
- Start with a predictable shot, then add configurable direction and power.
- Count a ball striking a stationary defending piece as a successful block.
  Report goals, misses, and blocks separately.
- A block-flick that touches the ball or an attacking piece is illegal: discard
  it and return play to the attacker.

## Possible follow-on drills

- **Pass-lane interception:** position a defender between the ball and a
  receiver, then test a scripted pass.
- **Shot selection:** vary ball position, angle, and shooting-area eligibility.
- **Moving attack:** add multiple attacking pieces or vary the shot lane after
  each block.
- **Goalkeeper practice:** consider a separate drill later because the
  goalkeeper is rod-controlled and follows different touch rules.

Reuse the scenario editor where practical, but keep the defensive objective
distinct from the attacking mode's goal of reaching a target area. Provide
feedback on block legality, lane coverage, and the outcome of the subsequent
shot.
